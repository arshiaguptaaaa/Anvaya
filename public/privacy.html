Privacy Policy

Effective 22 October 2026

Anvaya (“we”, “us”) runs career-preparation programmes, mentoring sessions and resume services through anvayacareers.in and app.anvayacareers.in. This policy explains what information we collect, why, who we share it with and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account and contact details: name, email address, phone number, college, programme or cohort, target roles and goals you share with us.
  • Documents you upload: resumes, LinkedIn content and assignment submissions.
  • Session information: booking times, attendance, feedback, and notes that mentors choose to share with you.
  • Payment status: amounts, plans and order references. Card, UPI and bank details are entered directly with Razorpay; we never see or store them.
  • Technical information: sign-in records, security logs and basic device and browser details needed to keep accounts secure.

How we use it

  • To provide your programme, sessions and services, and to give you access to what you have paid for.
  • To schedule sessions and send confirmations, reminders and changes by email.
  • To respond to support requests and to improve our programmes.
  • To keep the platform secure, prevent misuse and meet legal and accounting obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Google sign-in and Google Calendar

If you choose Continue with Google, we receive your name and email address to sign you in. Nothing else is requested.

Mentors and Anvaya staff who choose Connect Google Calendar allow Anvaya to:

  • read free/busy times from the calendars they select, so students are only offered times when the host is available (event titles and details are not read or shown);
  • list their calendars, so they can choose which ones to check and where sessions are added;
  • create, update and cancel Anvaya session events, with a Google Meet link, on the calendar they choose.

Anvaya’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide these scheduling features. It is not used for advertising, not sold, not used to train AI models, and not read by people except where needed for security, to comply with law, or with the user’s permission. A host can disconnect at any time from the Calendar page or from their Google Account permissions; we then delete the stored access token.

Who we share it with

We share information only as needed to run Anvaya, with:

  • Your mentors and the Anvaya team, for the sessions and services you take part in.
  • Service providers that process data for us: Google Cloud (hosting, sign-in, calendar and Meet), Supabase (database and file storage), Resend (email delivery), Razorpay (payments) and Vercel (website hosting).
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

Storage, security and retention

Data is stored with the providers above and protected with encryption in transit, access controls and audit logs. We keep account and session records for as long as your account is active and for a reasonable period afterwards for support, accounting and legal purposes. Then we delete or anonymise them.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your information, or withdraw consent for optional uses, by emailing connect@anvayacareers.in. We will respond within a reasonable time and in line with applicable Indian data-protection law. Some records may need to be kept for legal or accounting reasons.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version, and we will tell users by email about significant changes. For questions or concerns, email connect@anvayacareers.in.

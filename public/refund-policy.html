Refund & Rescheduling

Effective 26 October 2026

This policy applies to every Anvaya plan, programme and paid session, whether you bought it on the payment page or in your Anvaya dashboard. It is part of our Terms of Service.

In short
  • You can reschedule once per plan, if you tell us at least 24 hours before the session.
  • If you cancel, the fee isn’t refunded. Use your one reschedule instead.
  • If we cancel or can’t deliver what you paid for, you get a full refund.
  • A missed session, or a change asked for less than 24 hours before, counts as used.

Rescheduling

  • Each plan you buy includes one reschedule. For plans with several sessions, that is one reschedule across the whole plan, not one per session.
  • Ask at least 24 hours before the session starts. Use the reschedule link in your booking email, your Anvaya dashboard, or write to us at teamanvaya.in@gmail.com.
  • Once your reschedule is used, the new time is final.

If you cancel

  • Fees are not refunded when you cancel a plan or a session. If the time no longer suits you, please use your one reschedule (with 24 hours’ notice).

Missed sessions and late requests

  • If you miss a session, or ask to change it less than 24 hours before it starts, the session counts as used and isn’t refunded. The rest of your plan continues as normal.
  • In a genuine emergency, message us as soon as you can. We may offer to rebook it at our discretion.

If we cancel or can’t deliver

  • If Anvaya cancels a session or a plan, or can’t deliver what you paid for, you get a full refund for what wasn’t delivered. If you’d rather keep the plan, we’ll rebook instead. You choose.
  • If your host doesn’t join or a technical problem on our side stops the session, it doesn’t count against you. We’ll rebook it, or refund it if you prefer, and it doesn’t use up your reschedule.

Duplicate or incorrect payments

  • If you were charged twice for the same purchase, or charged more than the price shown, we refund the extra amount in full.

How refunds are paid

  • Refunds go back to the original payment method through Razorpay. We start the refund within 2 working days of agreeing it. Your bank usually takes a further 5–7 working days to show it.

Questions

Write to teamanvaya.in@gmail.com. Nothing in this policy affects any rights you have under Indian consumer law.

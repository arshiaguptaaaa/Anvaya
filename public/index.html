What’s the difference between the two plans? +

The Complete Resume Build & Rewrite (₹2,250, 3 × 45 min) covers the full resume — dissection, extraction, rewriting, and final build. The Full Picture — Profile Strategy + Resume (₹3,999, 3 × 60 min + a bonus 30 min) includes all of that, plus career direction, LinkedIn, application strategy, and a portfolio roadmap.

Is this only for freshers or recent graduates? +

No. The process works for students, freshers, working professionals, MBA applicants, career switchers, and anyone mid-search not hearing back. It adapts to where you are.

How is this different from a CV-writing service? +

We work 1:1 with you — building the resume together, live, in session. No templates. No AI. Every bullet is yours because you built it with us in real time. You can defend every line in an interview.

I don’t have numbers. Can this still work? +

Yes. Most people don’t walk in with ready metrics. That’s what the extraction steps are for. We derive numbers from what you actually did — team size, project count, client reach, revenue touched.

How do the sessions work? +

Every session is live and 1:1 on Google Meet. After paying, you pick your time; you’ll get a confirmation email with the Meet link and a short pre-session questionnaire. Multi-session plans are booked one session at a time, at a pace that suits you. Sessions are not recorded unless you ask and we both agree.

Can I reschedule? +

Yes, once per plan, as long as you tell us at least 24 hours before the session. Use the reschedule link in your booking email or write to teamanvaya.in@gmail.com. A session missed, or changed with less than 24 hours’ notice, counts as used. In a genuine emergency, message us and we’ll see what we can do.

Do you give refunds? +

If you cancel, the fee isn’t refunded — please use your one reschedule instead. If we cancel or can’t deliver what you paid for, you get a full refund to your original payment method. Full details: Refund & Rescheduling Policy.

What happens right after payment? +

You’re redirected to Calendly to book your first session immediately. A confirmation email with your booking link and the pre-session questionnaire is sent to your inbox.

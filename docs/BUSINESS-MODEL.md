# Anvaya business-model assessment (with Career Edge as a reference)

24 October 2026. Each statement is tagged:
- **[Fact]** — I read it on a public page or in our own records today.
- **[Their claim]** — a figure Career Edge publishes about itself; I can't check it.
- **[Inference]** — my reading of the evidence.
- **[Unknown]** — not public, or not in our data yet.

Nothing here estimates Career Edge's revenue, profit or conversion rates. Their site doesn't support that, and their testimonials aren't evidence of it.

## 1. Career Edge (career-edge.in), what is publicly visible

### Who they are
- **[Fact]** ISB-alumni-led. Co-founded in June 2021 by Anurag Yadav, Archit Maheshwari, Sarthak Verma and Sarthak Bhatt ([Poets&Quants, Feb 2022](https://poetsandquants.com/2022/02/15/meet-the-isb-grads-helping-indian-mba-students-thrive/)). Today the site presents Sarthak Verma as the face of the business: the About page, FAQ answers, "Founder Hours", WhatsApp sales and the YouTube channel (@sarthak-verma).
- **[Fact]** It started as a **1:1 mentor marketplace**: students picked an industry, got matched to a paid mentor, and paid "small, affordable fees". By February 2022 it reported 100+ mentors and nearly 1,000 mentees (Poets&Quants). Today the site leads with **cohort "fellowships"** instead.
  - **[Inference]** The 1:1 marketplace didn't scale or earn enough, so they moved to group programmes with higher prices and less delivery time per buyer.

### Who they sell to
- **[Their claim]** 65% of learners are working professionals. Most have 0–6 years' experience, though 25% have 10+. 30% are engineers and 20% management graduates ([Career Outcomes](https://career-edge.in/careeroutcomes/)).
- **[Fact]** The Consulting Fellowship takes undergraduates (6-month track) as well as master's students and professionals (2.5-month track) ([Consulting Fellowship](https://career-edge.in/consulting-fellowship/)).

### What they sell ([Career Programs](https://career-edge.in/career-programs/))

| Programme | Format [Fact] | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Consulting Fellowship | 2.5 or 6 months. Sunday Zoom 6–9 pm, Saturday "Founder Hours", self-paced work, 1:1 CV feedback, guesstimates, cases, a live client project and referrals | **Not published** |
| Venture Capital Fellowship | 2 months, Sunday Zoom 3–5 pm, recorded | **Not published** |
| MBA/MIM applications mentorship + GMAT | Unlimited 1:1 Zoom with an alum of the target school. GMAT live (daily or weekend) or self-paced; 10 mock tests | **Not published** |
| Product Management Fellowship | Named on the Programs page; no live link found | Unknown |

- **[Fact]** The only pricing statement is in the FAQ: the fellowship "has been intentionally priced at 1/5th of similar career programs". No fee appears on the site, and none turned up in a web search.
- **[Fact]** The refund policy says no refunds once paid, partial payments and token fees are allowed, and a token is forfeited if the balance isn't paid before the start date ([Refund Policy](https://career-edge.in/refundpolicy/)).
  - **[Inference]** They sell with a deposit and pay-the-rest-before-the-cohort-starts model.

### Proof and trust signals
- **[Their claims]**
  - 10K+ trained; 82% "made a shift"; ₹19L average CTC (range ₹12–45L); 100 mentors.
  - 80% placement "within 6 months for fellows who seek placement support & interview with firms"; 85% final admits; 92% NPS.
  - Featured in LinkedIn News 15+ times.
- **[Inference]** The consulting outcome figure only counts fellows who asked for referrals *and* got interviews, so it isn't a placement rate for everyone who enrols. Their FAQ shows the same narrowing: 40–50% within 2 months and 70–80% within 5 months, for that group only.
- **[Fact]** Every testimonial shows the person's name and current employer or admit, and many link to LinkedIn profiles. Mentor bios name current firms (e.g. "Junior Associate, McKinsey; ex-Bain"). A 2022 founder quote: "People trust the brand more than you as a person, so we're bringing on mentors from big companies."

### How a visitor becomes a buyer
- **[Fact]** Every programme ends at "Apply Now" or "Get Your Profile Evaluated". That leads to a **Google Form** asking name, WhatsApp number, email, current stage and track. The form tells you to save Sarthak Verma's number and "drop a Hi on WhatsApp" for a faster reply.
  - There's also a public WhatsApp community link and wa.me links in the site footer.
- **[Inference]** Selling is a founder-led WhatsApp conversation: qualify, then quote the price privately, then take a deposit. That works at small volume but depends on the founder's time, and it hides the price, which may filter out price-sensitive buyers or start a negotiation.
- **[Unknown]** How many applicants buy, what the price is, cohort sizes, refund disputes, and what mentors are paid.

### Where their customers come from
- **[Fact]**
  - Founder-led content: a personal YouTube channel, LinkedIn (the "LinkedIn News 15+ times" claim), Instagram and a WhatsApp community.
  - College reach: "impacted over 6,000 students at more than 20 Indian colleges" (2022).
  - Repeated proof: name-plus-employer testimonials.
- **[Unknown]** Paid ads spend; which channel actually produces buyers.

### What problem they make money from, and why people pay
- **[Inference]** A **high-stakes, one-off transition**: getting into consulting, VC or a top business school.
  - Candidates know the payoff is big. Their own pages mention ₹12–45L salaries and top-school admits.
  - They don't know the unwritten rules: CV format, guesstimates, cases, essays.
  - They lack insiders to practise with or get referred by.
- People pay for three things: **insider access** (a current MBB consultant or school alum), **structured practice with feedback**, and **referral access**. All are hard to get for free outside top colleges.

### Which offers look repeatable and scalable
- **[Inference]**
  - **Most scalable:** cohort teaching (one live session serves many) and recorded content.
  - **Middle:** templated 1:1 parts, such as CV feedback against a checklist.
  - **Least scalable:** Founder Hours, unlimited 1:1 essay mentoring, referrals (which depend on mentor goodwill), and the live client project (which needs a client every cohort).
- The design pushes scale into the shared parts and caps the personal parts (fixed weekly Founder Hours, buddy-team practice).

## 3. What Anvaya can learn

**Packaging**
- Name the transition, not the deliverable ("Get into consulting" rather than "Resume rewrite").
- Use a timeline with checkpoints and a fixed start date. That creates urgency without fake discounts.

**Trust**
- Use real names with the employer or admit, and link profiles where the person agrees.
- Show mentors with their current role.
- Publish outcome figures *with the group they're measured on*. Career Edge's narrowed denominator is a warning as much as a template: if we publish a number, state exactly whom it covers.

**Conversion**
- Their form, then WhatsApp, then founder flow gets a high-intent conversation.
- We already have a lower-friction route (free chat, public price, pay online). What we lack is the **personal follow-up**. The CRM's Checkouts page and free-chat list now show who to follow up; someone has to do it.

**Acquisition**
- Their acquisition is founder-led content plus college sessions plus visible proof. None of that needs ad spend; it needs consistency.

## 4. Where Anvaya can differentiate rather than copy

- **[Inference]**
  1. **Transparent, small-ticket entry.** Our ₹500 call and public prices with instant checkout are the opposite of a hidden price and a WhatsApp negotiation. Keep it, and make the ₹500 call the default first step into bigger programmes.
  2. **Resume-first, evidence-based.** "500 resumes reviewed" and a Resume Intelligence Framework (our docs) is a narrower, more credible promise than "100 MBB mentors". Compete on depth of CV work, not mentor logos we don't have.
  3. **Honest outcomes.** Publish the denominator by default, as a deliberate contrast.
  4. **A product they don't have:** opportunity tracking (the Shortlist Club roles lists) plus a student dashboard with session history.
- **Don't copy:** unlimited 1:1, a strict no-refund policy (it clashes with our trust positioning), or outcome claims we can't back.

## 5. What an ₹8,000/month intern can realistically do

- **[Inference]** Plan for a part-time student intern at roughly 15–20 hours a week. Bounded, checkable work only; no selling high-ticket programmes, no delivering CV edits on their own.

| Activity | Hours/week | Output you can check |
|---|---|---|
| Follow up leads within 24h (free chats, abandoned checkouts with consent, form enquiries) using approved message templates | 4–5 | Follow-ups logged in the CRM; replies |
| Turn your sessions and frameworks into posts (3/week), drafted for your approval | 4 | Posts published; profile visits and free chats from link codes |
| Book college or club sessions: build a list of 40 placement cells/consulting clubs, email or DM them, aim for 1 session a month | 3–4 | Contacts made, sessions booked, attendees, QR sign-ups |
| Collect consented testimonials (name + outcome + LinkedIn) from past clients | 1–2 | New proof items on the site |
| Shortlist Club research: find and post consulting/strategy roles weekly | 2–3 | Roles posted; members |
| Weekly numbers sheet: leads by source, free chats, paid orders, revenue (from the CRM/admin) | 1 | One sheet every Monday |

- **[Fact-based arithmetic]** At our ₹2,250 average sale, covering the intern alone takes about **3.6 sales a month**. Covering the intern plus your ₹1,000–5,000 of fixed costs takes **4.1–5.9 sales a month**.
  - We've averaged about 1.6 a month, and none since July.
  - **So the intern only pays off if their work brings in at least 3–4 extra sales a month.** Judge them on that, starting with college sessions, the one channel that has produced every sale so far.

## 7. Three ideas worth testing

**Run these in the order 3 → 1 → 2.** All 4 paying customers came from college or club sessions, the only channel with proven sales. Test 3 scales it with the intern, Test 1 gives those audiences a group product to buy, and Test 2 improves the follow-up step.

### Test 1 — "Consulting Sprint" cohort with a public price
- **What:** 4 Sundays × 2 hours, 6–12 seats, **₹2,499** shown on the page.
  - Content: guesstimates and case basics, plus a 1:1 CV checklist pass.
  - Uses the existing Guesstimate material, sold through the existing payment page as a new plan.
- **Cost and effort:**
  - About 8 live hours plus 6–8 prep hours from you.
  - About 10 intern hours on promotion and reminders.
  - About 1 hour of my work to add the plan.
  - ₹0 ads.
- **Evidence it works:**
  - **6 or more paid seats** within 14 days of announcing it to our existing leads and followers.
  - **At least 70% attendance** across the 4 sessions.
  - **2 or more** participants buying a 1:1 programme afterwards.
- **Stop or rethink:** fewer than 4 seats, or attendance under 50%.

### Test 2 — The ₹500 call as the deliberate first step
- **What:** promote the MBA Planning call (and a matching consulting version) as the main "start here" option. Follow every buyer up within 48 hours with a written plan and one recommended next programme.
- **Cost and effort:**
  - About 30 minutes per call, plus 15 minutes for the written plan.
  - About 3 intern hours a week for follow-up.
  - ₹0 ads.
- **Evidence it works:** over 6–8 weeks, **≥20 ₹500 calls** booked, and **≥25% of them** buying a ₹1,500+ programme within 30 days, measured in the CRM by email.
  - These thresholds are mine; set yours from what's normal for you.

### Test 3 — College and club sessions run by the intern
- **What:** the intern books one free 45-minute "Guesstimate / CV teardown" session a month with a college consulting club or placement cell. A QR code at the end goes to the free 15-minute chat.
- **Cost and effort:**
  - About 1.5 hours of your time per session.
  - About 4 intern hours a week on outreach.
  - ₹0–₹500 for materials.
- **Evidence it works, per session:**
  - **≥25 attendees** and **≥5 free-chat bookings**.
  - **≥1 paid order within 30 days** from those attendees, tracked with a dedicated link code on the free-chat form.
- **Stop or rethink:** two sessions in a row with no paid orders.
